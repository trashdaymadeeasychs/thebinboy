Privacy policy

The Bin Boy is a local bin cleaning business in Charleston, SC. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and who we share it with.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us:

  • When you book a cleaning: your name, service address, phone number, email, bin count, and the plan you choose.
  • When you request a commercial quote: your name, business or property name, phone number, email, property type, bin or dumpster count, and anything you write in the notes.
  • When you call, text, or email us: your phone number or email address and what you tell us.

This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics, and it does not set cookies of its own.

How we use it

  • To schedule and carry out your bin cleanings.
  • To answer your questions and send you quotes.
  • To let you know when we're on the way and when we're done, and to send reminders and invoices for your service.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Services we use

A few outside services help us run the business. They receive only what they need to do their job:

  • Jobber runs our online booking, client portal, scheduling, and invoicing. Booking links open Jobber's site, which has its own privacy policy.
  • EmailJS delivers the commercial quote form to our inbox.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve the fonts and some code this site uses. Like any website your browser loads files from, they can see your IP address.

Links to Facebook, Instagram, Google reviews, and our partner Trash Day Made Easy take you to their sites, which follow their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep customer details for as long as you use our service, and afterward only as long as we need them for records such as invoices. If you only asked for a quote and didn't sign up, ask us and we'll delete your details.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you.
  • Tell us if you'd rather not get texts or emails, and we'll stop anything that isn't needed for a cleaning you've booked.

Questions about your information? Email bryan@thebinboy.com or call (843) 478-0108.

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top of the page.
