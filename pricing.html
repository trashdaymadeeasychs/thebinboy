A few common questions

How does bi-weekly pricing work?

Bi-weekly service includes two cleanings per month for one bin. The first cleaning is $25 and the second is $15, for a total of $40 for the two cleanings.

What does bi-monthly mean?

Bi-monthly service is one cleaning every other month for $35, with one bin included. It is different from the bi-weekly plan, which includes two cleanings per month.

Are commitments required?

No. No commitments are required for these service options.

How much is an additional trash or recycling bin?

One bin is included in each listed plan. All additional bins are $15 per bin.

Which schedules are available at my address?

Route availability depends on your neighborhood. Check our service area, then call or book online to discuss your address and preferred schedule.
