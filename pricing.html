Which plan fits your home?

The monthly plan at $20 per month is a practical choice if smells and buildup return quickly. Monthly availability depends on the routes and demand in your neighborhood.

The quarterly plan at $40 per quarter gives you one cleaning every three months. It suits households that want regular help with bin cleaning on a less frequent schedule.

A single visit at $60 covers a one-time cleaning of two bins. It's an option when you want to try the service or take care of a bin that needs attention without choosing a recurring plan.

Have more than two bins, multiple properties, or a shared trash area? Contact us about your setup. Commercial bins and dumpsters are quoted separately.

A few common questions

Is monthly service available at my address?

Monthly routes depend on customer count and demand in your area. Check the service-area page, then call or book online to discuss the options for your address.

Are there long-term contracts?

Monthly customers are billed monthly with no long-term contracts and can cancel at any time.

Do I need a separate plan for my recycling bin?

Each listed residential plan includes two bins. If you need more than two cleaned, contact us for a custom quote.
