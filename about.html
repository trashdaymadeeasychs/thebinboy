The Bin Boy · Charleston, SC

Your local bin cleaning crew

Bryan Rojas started The Bin Boy in 2021. Today we serve homeowners across Charleston and the surrounding Lowcountry, taking care of the dirty bins so you have one less job waiting in the driveway.

A simple service for an unpleasant chore

Food scraps, bag leaks, and everyday use leave buildup in garbage and recycling bins. A quick rinse often leaves the smell behind. Our job is to bring the equipment and do the cleaning for you.

We come to your home with a purpose-built mobile cleaning rig, wash your bins inside and out at 200°, and apply an odor-neutralizing solution. Then we return your bins to your garage or the location you prefer.

The service is built around convenience: you don't need to be home, you don't need to provide a water hookup, and we'll text you when we're on the way and when we're done. Choose a single cleaning or put repeat visits on the calendar.
