A few common questions

Does the cleaning truck need my water hookup?

No. Our mobile cleaning unit is self-contained. We bring the equipment needed to wash your bins at your property.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave your bins at the curb or driveway where our team can reach them. Read what to expect on cleaning day.

How much does cleaning cost?

The residential plans are $20 monthly, $40 quarterly, or $60 for a single visit, with two bins included. See the plans and route-availability details.
