# Google Business Profile checklist: The Bin Boy

Your Google Business Profile (the listing that shows in Google Maps and in the
box beside search results) is likely the biggest driver of local "near me"
searches. Work through this once, then keep up the weekly habits at the end.

Edit the profile at **business.google.com**, or search "The Bin Boy" on Google
while signed in to the owner account and use the edit options shown there.

> **Note:** Google changes this dashboard often, so some menu names below may
> differ from what you see. Where I'm unsure a feature still exists, it's marked.

---

## 3. Categories

The **primary category** matters most for which searches you show up in.

- [ ] Type "bin", "trash can" and "pressure washing" into the category box and

      or cleaning category, if offered). Don't add categories for services you
      don't provide.

## 6. Photos

Real photos of your work help people trust you more than anything you write.

- [ ] **Logo:** use the round Bin Boy mascot logo.
- [ ] **Cover photo:** the before/after bin photo or a clear shot of the truck.
- [ ] **At least 10 photos to start:**
  - before/after shots
  - the truck and cleaning rig
  - Bryan and the crew at work
  - bins being returned to a garage
- [ ] **Use your own photos only**: no stock images or text-heavy graphics.
- [ ] **Add 2 to 3 new photos every week** from real jobs, in different
      towns when you can.

## 7. Posts

Posts show on your profile and signal that the business is active.

- [ ] **Post once a week.** Ideas:
  - a before/after photo from that week's route ("Summerville bins, cleaned
    this morning")
  - the first-cleaning offer with the STINKY code
  - seasonal reminders (summer heat and flies, holiday leftovers)
  - commercial dumpster jobs
- [ ] **Add a "Book" or "Learn more" button** linking to the booking page or
      `https://thebinboy.com/pricing.html`.

## 8. Reviews

You have a strong rating; keep new reviews coming steadily.

- [ ] **Get your review link** (look for "Ask for reviews" or "Get more
      reviews" in the dashboard) and save it.
  - **Send that link to Claude:** the website's "Read reviews on Google"
    buttons currently use a Google search link. Your profile's own link is
    more reliable, and I can swap it in.
- [ ] **Ask every new customer**, for example in Jobber's follow-up message
      after their first cleaning.
- [ ] **Follow Google's rules:**
  - **Ask everyone**, not only happy customers.
  - **Don't offer discounts or rewards** for reviews.
  - **Don't write reviews for yourself.**
- [ ] **Reply to every review within a few days**, good or bad. Thank people
      by name, and where it's natural, mention the service and town, e.g.
      "Glad your Mount Pleasant bins are fresh again."
- [ ] **For a negative review:** reply calmly once, offer to make it right,
      and give your phone number.

## 9. Other settings

- [ ] **Attributes:** turn on any that apply, such as "Onsite services".
      Only claim what's true.
- [ ] **Questions and answers:** if this section still appears on your
      profile (Google has been phasing it out, as far as I know), add and
      answer 3 to 5 common questions yourself, e.g. "Do I need to be home?"
      and "Do you clean recycling bins too?"
- [ ] **Verification:** make sure the profile shows as verified. If Google
      asks you to re-verify, do it promptly.
- [ ] **Managers:** add a second trusted Google account as an owner or
      manager so you're never locked out.

## 10. Weekly habits (about 15 minutes)

- [ ] 2 to 3 new job photos
- [ ] 1 post
- [ ] Reply to every new review
- [ ] Check for suggested edits from Google or the public, and reject any
      that are wrong.

## 11. Monthly check

- [ ] Look at the profile's performance numbers (calls, website clicks,
      direction requests and searches) to see what's working.
- [ ] Confirm the hours, prices and service areas still match the website.
