Getting ready for your cleaning

Leave your trash and recycling bins at the curb or driveway where our team can reach them. You don't need to be home for the visit. If you have questions about access to your property, call Bryan before your cleaning.

We bring a self-contained cleaning rig, so there is no water hookup needed at your home. Your bins get a 200° hot water wash and an odor-neutralizing solution. Once the work is done, we return them to your garage or your preferred location.

We'll text you when we're on the way and when we're done. For repeat visits, choose a recurring plan so bin cleaning stays on the schedule without becoming another chore.

A few common questions

What is included in a visit?

Every residential plan includes one bin, a 200° wash, odor-neutralizing solution, and bins returned to your garage. Additional bins are $15 per bin. See current plans and pricing.

Can I book just one cleaning?

Yes. A single visit is $80 for one bin, with additional bins at $15 per bin. Bi-weekly, monthly, bi-monthly, and quarterly options are available for recurring cleanings.

Do you clean commercial bins too?

Yes. Commercial cleaning covers bins, dumpsters, and shared trash areas with a quote based on the property and cleaning need.
